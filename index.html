Established 2008 · Kampala, Uganda

Built here.
Operating everywhere.

Market Bolt was founded by marketing professionals who believed Ugandan brands deserved world-class activation — not a second-tier version of it. We started with conviction, a handful of clients, and fifteen years of industry experience between us.

Today we operate countrywide, with regional reach across East Africa and partnerships with international institutions. We began in the field and continue to build in the digital space — both with the same precision. From field teams in Gulu to digital platforms in Kampala, from door-to-door distribution routes to data-driven loyalty programmes — on the ground and online, doing the work.

The brands that took a chance on us in 2008 are still calling. That is not luck. That is delivery.
